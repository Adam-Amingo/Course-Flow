# CourseFlow KNUST Edition - User Guide

Welcome to **CourseFlow**, your central academic portal for managing coursework, checking class schedules, and interacting with your lecturers and students. 

This guide will explain how to use the system whether you are a Student or a Lecturer at KNUST.

---

## 1. Getting Started (Login)

Access the portal from the main login screen. The system determines your role automatically based on your KNUST Institutional email format:

*   **For Students:** 
    *   Use an email ending in `@st.knust.edu.gh`
    *   *Example Account:* `aabubakar@st.knust.edu.gh`
*   **For Lecturers:**
    *   Use an email ending in `@lc.knust.edu.gh`
    *   *Example Account:* `linda@lc.knust.edu.gh`

---

## 2. Student Portal Guide

When you log in as a student, you will be taken to the **Student Dashboard**.

### The Dashboard
*   **Quick Metrics:** At the top, view your overall Academic Progress, Upcoming Deadlines, classes for the week, and active Announcements.
*   **Enrolled Courses (My Courses):** Click on any course card to inspect specific details, view course material, or check assignments.
*   **Notice Board:** View recent notices broadcasted by your lecturers or by the administration. Look out for "Urgent" vs "Info" tags.

### Inside a Course (Course Detail View)
Once you click a course, you have three main tabs:
1.  **Resources Tab:** View and download files uploaded by the lecturer (PDFs, Images, Code snippets, etc.).
2.  **Assignments Tab:** A quick list view of upcoming, pending, or submitted assignments.
3.  **Kanban Board:** A Drag-and-Drop styled board to help you manage your tasks. 
    *   Click the **"+"** button under "To Do" to add your own personal tasks.
    *   Move assignments to "In Progress" when you are working on them.
    *   *(Note: Submitting is currently simulated by moving a task to "Done")*

### Focus Mode ⚡
When it's time to study, click **Focus Mode** (top right of the Course page) to enter a distraction-free Pomodoro timer interface to help you concentrate!

## 3. Lecturer Portal Guide

When you log in as a lecturer, you will be redirected to the **Manager / Lecturer Dashboard**.

### The Dashboard
*   **My Teaching Courses:** See an overview of the classes you instruct, including total student count and when the next class is held.
*   **Broadcast Notice:** Use the large button on your dashboard to instantly publish an announcement to all students across your courses.
*   **Class Performance:** A quick glance at how your students are doing across the board (On Track vs Review Needed).

### Managing a Course (Lecturer Course View)
Clicking on any of your teaching courses opens the detailed management view:

1.  **Create Assignment:**
    *   Click the **Create Assignment** button at the top right.
    *   Fill out the Title, Due Date, and select whether it is a "Project" or an "Exam".
    *   Click **Publish Assignment**. This makes it immediately visible on the students' Kanban boards.
  
2.  **Tracking Submissions:**
    *   Under **Active Assignments**, you'll see a card for every assignment you've created, along with a completion progress circle.
    *   **Click an Assignment Card** to expand the table.
    *   The expanded view shows every single student's status (`Submitted`, `Pending`, `Late`), their grade, and allows you to simulate sending a Grade Report or sending a Reminder to late students.
